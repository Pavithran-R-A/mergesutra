# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone who
participates in this project, regardless of experience level, gender identity
and expression, body size, disability, ethnicity, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Standards

Behaviours that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Fake accounts, purchased stars, bot voting, or any engagement manipulation

Competition outcomes must come from product quality, usefulness, documentation,
demo clarity, and legitimate community outreach — never manipulation.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers. Reports are reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and will be kept confidential.
